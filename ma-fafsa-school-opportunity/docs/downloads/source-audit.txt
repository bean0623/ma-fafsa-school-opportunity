OUTCOME YEARS AND INTERPRETATION

Graduation: 2025, All Students, four-year rate; all seven saved fields match for
401 schools. This is a graduation cohort measure.

College attendance: 2024–25 graduates, All Students, all colleges/universities;
graduate counts match for 405 and attendance/category fields for 381. The source
header does not establish the college-enrollment follow-up window. The 24 blank
attendance observations are missing, not zero.

MassCore: 2024–25, All Students; all three saved fields match for 393 schools.
Completion percentage refers to graduates.

MCAS: 2025 testing year; all saved subject fields match (ELA 370 schools, math 367,
science 354). The supplied workbook does not explicitly record grade selection
or subgroup. 2025 testing is not a Class of 2025 graduation-cohort measure.
If grade 10 is confirmed, it generally describes the cohort graduating in 2027;
a Class of 2025 grade-10 comparison would need 2023 testing data. Use the 2025
measure as school context until those filters are confirmed.

AP participation: 2024–25, All Subjects, All Students; unique exam takers match for
336 schools, and detailed exam-count fields for 327. This counts exam takers,
not AP-course enrollment. The precise included grade universe is not documented
in the supplied header. Do not divide by grade-12 enrollment or graduation cohort.
An enrollment-based AP-taker rate needs a verified aligned grade population.

AP performance: 2024–25, All Subjects, All Students; exams taken match for 336,
score categories/percentages for 328. Percent scoring 3–5 uses exams as denominator;
one student may contribute several exams. It is not percent of all students.

Attendance: 2024–25 end of year, All Students; 413 source matches and 412 numerical
records. The two uploaded attendance versions are cell-for-cell identical.
Attendance/chronic absence are whole-school measures; grade spans differ.

SAT: 2024–25, All Students; 366 schools with test counts, 334 with scores.
Scores describe participating test takers. Grade/cohort selection is not explicit
in the supplied header, and results are not universal student achievement.

Graduate plans: 2024–25; 405 graduate-plan counts and 393 category percentages
match. Plans are intentions, not observed college enrollment. The header does
not separately document a student subgroup selection.

Pathways enrollment: 2025–26; totals/percent/all-student counts match for 215 schools,
grade-specific fields for 205. These are program-participation counts, not ordinary
grade enrollment. Keep them separate from the 2024–25 main outcome comparison
unless obtaining a matching earlier source or explicitly presenting later context.

Across all inspected raw outcome files, there were no numerical disagreements
with populated corresponding DTA values. Availability differs by outcome because
source coverage and suppression differ; regression samples must show their N.

ACS CAMPUS-TRACT CONTEXT

The workbook has 1,620 unique 11-digit tract GEOIDs. All 411 single-campus tract
assignments in the DTA are present: known income/poverty/education/rent values match
numerically. One income and 23 rent sentinel cells (-666666666) were correctly
converted to missing. Six assigned schools have the source income value 250001;
treat that as a possible top-coded category requiring source documentation.

Four multiple-campus schools match unweighted arithmetic averages of tract values
(Brooke, Kennedy Academy, Hampden Charter and Roxbury Prep). These are averages of
tract medians, not a pooled student or neighborhood household-income median.

The filename '2024' alone does not prove ACS release/vintage: the supplied workbook
does not contain official retrieval URLs, ACS table IDs, formulas, margins of error
or vintage documentation. Numerical matching does not verify campus geocoding or
that students live in the school neighborhood. Use campus-tract income as context,
with primary SES grouping based on the school student low-income share.
